HOW IT WORKS

SELECT: click ship parts or use tree. PAINT: left-drag on a surface; right-drag to rotate. MOVE/ROTATE/SCALE: select a part then use its gizmo. Press Ctrl+Z / Ctrl+Shift+Z to undo/redo.

FBX and OBJ should first be converted to GLB. Models with separate named hull parts are easier to edit accurately than a single merged mesh.
