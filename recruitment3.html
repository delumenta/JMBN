Before You Join

QUICK QUESTIONS.

Everything you need to know before saying hello.

What kind of organisation is JMBN?

JMBN is a casual but organised mil-sim organisation in Star Citizen. We enjoy coordinated crew operations, clear communication and teamwork, while keeping the atmosphere relaxed and real-life commitments first.

Do I need experience, a ship or a specific role?

No. New players are welcome. You don't need a particular ship, rank or career path to introduce yourself.

When do you play?

We usually meet every Saturday from 8 PM Singapore time (GMT+8). If a session changes, we'll share an update in Discord.

Where are members based?

Our members are based in Singapore, Malaysia and Australia. We welcome interested players from Southeast Asia and Australia.

What do you expect from members?

Clear communication, thoughtful play, cooperation and looking out for one another. We know our members have lives beyond the 'verse.
