ARCADE MODE

Man your Polaris turret. WEPS will issue orders and change weapons status. Find contacts on the radar, aim with your mouse, turn with A/D or ←/→, press T to lock and automatically track it, transmit reports with 1–6, and hold Space to fire. Wrong actions are carried out — and have consequences.
