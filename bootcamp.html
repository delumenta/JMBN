BOOTCAMP 1.0

BASIC CREW TRAINING

A practical introduction to operating together aboard our capital ship,
on the ground, and as a unified crew.

◎ Objective

Build a common foundation for all JMBN members — how we operate, communicate and work together, from ship to ground.

☑ Outcome

By the end of Bootcamp 1.0, you will:

  • Understand how JMBN operates
  • Know what is expected of you
  • Operate effectively in a ground team
  • Function as part of a Polaris crew
  • Communicate and coordinate with the wider crew

ⓘ Details

  • ♟Open to all members
    (New and existing)
  • ◷~ 2.5–3 hours
  • ⌖Aboard the JMBN Polaris
    (Training Session)
  • ◇Casual Milsim / Practical Training
    (No pass or fail)

▣ Training Programme

01.

Induction & Etiquette

◷ ≈ 20 min

  • How we play (casual milsim, teamwork, camaraderie)
  • Punctuality & preparation (be ready, we won’t wait)
  • Communication etiquette (clear, concise, relevant)
  • Teamwork, initiative and expectations
  • Real life comes first (let us know if you need to leave)
02.

Ground Coordination

◷ ≈ 45 min

  • Basic formations (box, file, wedge)
  • Team movement (move, cover, regroup)
  • Contact callouts (spot, report, engage)
  • Friendly identification (avoid friendly fire)
  • Casualty recovery (revive and extract)
03.

Capital Ship Coordination

◷ ≈ 45 min

  • Crew roles & responsibilities (bridge, gunnery, engineering, etc.)
  • Polaris familiarisation (layout, key areas, access points)
  • Crew states (Standby vs Battlestations)
  • Bridge & crew communication (standard callouts)
  • Deployment & recovery (launch, retrieve and account for crew)
04.

Combined Exercise

◷ ≈ 30–45 min

  • A practical mission that brings everything together
  • Operate from the Polaris (staging, deployment, support)
  • Ground team completes an objective (e.g. bunker, outpost)
  • Coordination, communication and recovery

♟ Crew States

STANDBY

Relaxed state. Free to move around, socialise, prepare your equipment and familiarise yourself with the ship.

BATTLESTATIONS

Operational state. Report to your assigned station and keep communications clear and relevant.

» Next Steps

After Bootcamp 1.0, members can take part in follow-up training such as Engineering, Medical, Anti-Boarding and other specialised roles.
