One organization / Many paths

Where could you fit?

We train for practical roles so each member can contribute to the crew. Explore a path that interests you, learn alongside others, and try another specialisation as your interests grow.

01 / SHIP DEFENCE

Combat Systems Operator

Be the ship’s eyes, ears and first line of defence. Track threats, share clear information and help your crew make fast decisions under pressure.

02 / SHIP SYSTEMS

Electronic Technician

Keep the ship operational. Work with power and onboard systems, respond when things go wrong and help the crew stay in the fight.

03 / FIELD OPERATIONS

Special Warfare Crewman

Deploy with a small team for reconnaissance, boarding and ground operations. Coordinate closely, cover your crew and carry the mission forward.

Our approach

Progress is shared.

You do not need to arrive as an expert. Our Academy gives new members a place to learn the basics, while experienced crew can share what they know. Progress comes from practising together and helping the next person improve.
