01 — The idea

More than
a callsign.

JMBN takes inspiration from real-world naval crews. We bring those roles into Star Citizen through coordinated multi-crew play: pilots, gunners, engineers and ground teams working together toward the same mission.

Our structure helps everyone know their role when it matters, while training and shared missions give each member room to learn. What counts most is how we work together as a crew.

02 — The journey

Master
your role.

Every crewmate has something to contribute. Whether you fly, operate turrets, manage systems or support the team on the ground, there is room to learn and improve.

Train alongside the crew, discover the stations you enjoy and build the confidence to take on greater responsibility together.

03 — The mission

Make it
count.

A strong crew is built through shared experiences. From coordinated operations to spontaneous adventures, every member plays a part in making the mission possible.

Show up, support one another and make each deployment a story worth remembering.
