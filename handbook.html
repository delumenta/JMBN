01 / WELCOME ABOARD

Welcome to JMBN.

JMBN is a casual mil-sim Star Citizen organisation built around teamwork, immersion and camaraderie. We enjoy coordinated play without turning the game into a second job.

Hilarity · Immersion · Teamwork — and the shenanigans in between.

Whether you're new to the 'verse or an experienced citizen, what matters most is your willingness to communicate, learn and look out for the crew.

02 / THE JMBN WAY

How we run the 'verse.

Fall in. Close up. Make a story worth coming back for.

One crew

Look out for one another. Nobody has to tackle the 'verse alone.

Coordinated play

Clear communication, sensible decisions and shared objectives.

Casual mil-sim

Organised, not rigid. We value immersion without unnecessary formality.

Real life first

Join when you can. School, work and family come first.

03 / ROLES & STRUCTURE

Everyone has a place.

From pilots and gunners to engineers, logistics and ground teams, different skills help the crew succeed. Roles provide clarity during an activity, not a measure of someone's worth.

Specific ranks, appointments and reporting arrangements can be documented here as the organisation finalises them.

04 / CREW EXPECTATIONS

Look out for one another.

Respect the crew

Be considerate of other players, their time and their experience.

Communicate

Share useful information, listen to instructions and speak up when you need help.

Play together

Make room for different experience levels and help newcomers find their footing.

Keep perspective

We take teamwork seriously, but this is still a game. Real life comes first.

05 / OPERATIONS & SESSIONS

Form up. Head out.

We usually meet on Saturdays from 8 PM Singapore time (SGT, GMT+8). Activities can include coordinated operations, exploration, logistics, security and spontaneous adventures.

We form up, agree on the plan, establish roles and adapt together when things inevitably go sideways. Some of the best moments aren't planned.

No pressure to attend every session. Real life comes first.
06 / SPECIALISATIONS

Find your path.

Explore the parts of Star Citizen you enjoy most, build confidence and help others along the way.

Operations & security

Flight, ground coordination, escort and protection.

Logistics & industry

Transport, cargo, support and industrial gameplay.

Exploration

Discover new places, scout routes and support expeditions.

Support

Medical, recovery and the practical work that keeps everyone moving.
