ACADEMY

Welcome to JMBN.

You already know the basics of Star Citizen. You've flown ships, fought battles, completed missions, and learned the game through experience.

Now, you'll learn how to operate as part of a team. The JMBN Academy isn't here to teach you how to play the game. It's here to teach you how we operate together — our procedures, communication, formations, roles, and the standards expected when working as a JMBN unit.
WE DON'T TEACH YOU HOW TO PLAY STAR CITIZEN.
WE TEACH YOU HOW JMBN PLAYS STAR CITIZEN.
Your journey begins with Basic Military Training (BMT) and continues into specialised certifications as you develop your role within the organization.
