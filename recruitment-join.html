JMBN / Common questions

A few things to know.

Who are we?

JMBN is a casual mil-sim organization in Star Citizen, built around immersive crew operations and shared experiences.

What are we looking for?

We welcome Star Citizen players from Southeast Asia and Australia who are interested in being part of an organization. Students, working adults and parents are all welcome here.

How do we play?

We take part in coordinated, immersive gameplay. Teamwork, camaraderie and clear communication matter, and we look out for one another during operations.

When do we play?

We usually meet every Saturday from 8 pm Singapore time (GMT+8). If a session changes, we’ll share an update in Discord.

Where are we based?

Our members are based in Singapore, Malaysia and Australia.

Why do we play?

The ’verse is better with a crew. The adventure is more fun when you can count on others—and know someone has your back when things go sideways.
