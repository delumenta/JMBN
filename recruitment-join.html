Who we're looking for

Play as a crew.
Grow as a crew.

We value cooperation, consideration and camaraderie. We enjoy practical, immersive crew play, but we know members have lives beyond the game. Students, working adults and parents are all welcome here.

You do not have to be the best pilot or shot. We are looking for people who communicate, share the work and enjoy making the whole crew better.

New players are welcome. You do not need a particular ship, rank or career path to introduce yourself.

How to join

Three simple steps.

  2. 02Introduce yourself to the crew
  3. 03Find your first activity together

Tell us your callsign and what you enjoy doing in Star Citizen. A organization member can help you find your place and help you access the member login when you're ready.
