Who we're looking for

Play as a crew.
Grow as a crew.

We value cooperation, consideration and camaraderie. We enjoy practical, immersive crew play, but we know members have lives beyond the game. Students, working adults and parents are all welcome here.

You don’t have to be the best pilot or shot. We value clear communication, teamwork, thoughtful play, and looking out for the crew instead of rushing in alone.

New players are welcome. You don’t need a particular ship, rank or career path to introduce yourself.
