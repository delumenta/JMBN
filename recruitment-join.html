Who we're looking for

Play as a crew.
Grow as a crew.

We’re a casual mil-sim organization that meets every Saturday from 8 pm Singapore time (GMT+8). We value teamwork, respect and camaraderie, while making room for life outside the game.

New players are welcome. You don’t need a particular ship, rank or career path—just a willingness to communicate and work as a crew.

You do not have to be the best pilot or shot. We are looking for people who communicate, share the work and enjoy making the whole crew better.

How to join

  2. 02Introduce yourself to the crew
  3. 03Find your first activity together
